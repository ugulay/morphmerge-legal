Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game MorphMerge: Slime Merge Puzzle ("the App") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Android version distributed on Google Play.

1. Who we are

The App is made by Uğur Gülay, an individual developer based in Türkiye (publishing as BuildByteLogic), who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact: ugurgulay@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • There is no MorphMerge account. You never give us your name, email address or location.
  • Your scores, settings, progress, collection and purchases record are saved only on your device. We run no server, and the App sends nothing to us.
  • The App uses two Google services: Google Play Billing for purchases, and Google AdMob for ads. Those services receive the limited data described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised.

3. What is handled, and by whom

The App does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Its only device permissions are vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings), internet access for the Google services above, and the advertising ID.

4. Ads

Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch one (for example for a second chance or for free gems). In addition, after some finished games the App may show an ad between games; buying Remove ads stops those, while rewarded ads remain available as a choice. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and its partners: how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Sharing a result

If you tap Share on a result screen, the App draws a score card image on your device and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app to share to; what happens next is up to that app.

6. Your choices

  • Ad consent: in regions where consent is required, the App shows Google's consent form before ads start. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ad and privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android's settings (Privacy → Ads).
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the App, or clear its storage in Android's settings (Apps → MorphMerge → Storage → Clear storage). Because we keep no copy, this deletes everything we could ever hold about you. Purchases of permanent items can be restored afterwards with Restore purchases in the shop, on the same Google account.

7. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Türkiye)

Data is handled to provide the game and the purchases you request (performance of a contract), to keep ads working and free of fraud (legitimate interests), and, where the law requires it, on the basis of your consent (personalised ads). Under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) the same purposes apply; see the Turkish version for the full notice.

8. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell or rent data, and we have no data of yours to sell. The services above are provided by Google LLC and its affiliates, which may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

9. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Google keeps the data it receives according to its own policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or KVKK), you may ask to access, correct or delete data about you, object to or restrict its use, or withdraw consent. As we hold no data about you, most of these are exercised in the App and in Android's settings (section 6) or with Google. For anything else, email ugurgulay@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK Board).

11. Children

MorphMerge is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will help.

12. Security

Game data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google's services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

13. Changes

If the App starts handling data in a new way (for example a cloud save or analytics), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
