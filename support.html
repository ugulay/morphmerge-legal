Frequently asked

How do I play?

Drag to aim and release to drop a slime into the box. Two equal slimes merge into the next one; three in a touching row fuse two steps up. Do not let slimes rest above the red line for long. Tilt, shake and turn the box with the buttons at the bottom.

I bought something on another phone. How do I get it back?

Open the shop in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. Gem packs are used up when delivered and are not restored.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play: request a refund.

My progress is gone after reinstalling.

MorphMerge saves everything on your phone. Uninstalling, clearing the app's storage or moving to a new phone starts a new game; purchases can be restored as above.

The game runs slowly on my phone.

Open Settings and choose a lower Quality (Low or Medium), or Automatic.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Settings → Privacy → Ad and privacy choices (shown where consent is required).

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android's settings. See the Privacy Policy.
